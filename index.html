MBBS Course Details

The Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) is the primary undergraduate medical programme designed to build a strong foundation in medical sciences and clinical practice. The course equips students with comprehensive theoretical understanding and practical training in areas such as anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, surgery, and patient care. Through rigorous academic learning and hospital-based clinical exposure, students develop the essential skills required to diagnose illnesses and deliver effective treatment.

Karnataka is home to several prestigious medical institutions that offer world-class education, advanced clinical training, and extensive hands-on exposure, preparing students to excel in the ever-evolving healthcare industry. For students aspiring to pursue a career in medicine after qualifying NEET-UG, understanding the MBBS course structure, eligibility criteria, and admission process is crucial.

MBBS Syllabus

The MBBS syllabus in India is designed to provide students with a strong foundation in medical sciences along with extensive clinical training. The 5.5-year programme consists of 4.5 years of academic study divided into multiple phases, followed by a one-year compulsory rotatory internship that offers hands-on experience across various medical departments.

The curriculum is structured to help students develop in-depth knowledge of human anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology, surgery, and clinical medicine while strengthening their diagnostic and patient-care skills.

FAQ

Frequently
Asked
Questions

What is the MBBS course duration?
+
The MBBS course duration in India is 5.5 years, which includes 4.5 years of academic study followed by a 1-year compulsory rotatory internship with practical clinical training across multiple medical departments.
What is the eligibility criteria for MBBS admission?
+
Candidates must have completed 10+2 / 2nd PUC / equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as compulsory subjects. They must also secure the minimum qualifying marks specified for their category and qualify NEET-UG for admission.
Is NEET mandatory for MBBS admission?
+
Yes, NEET-UG is compulsory for admission to MBBS colleges in India. Without qualifying NEET, candidates are not eligible to participate in MBBS counselling or secure admission to medical colleges.
What subjects are covered in the MBBS syllabus?
+
The MBBS curriculum includes subjects such as Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry, Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Forensic Medicine, Community Medicine, General Medicine, General Surgery, Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, along with extensive clinical training.
What are the higher study options after MBBS?
+
After completing MBBS, graduates can pursue postgraduate medical programmes such as MD (Doctor of Medicine) or MS (Master of Surgery) in specialised branches like Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, Dermatology, Radiology, General Surgery, Orthopaedics, and Obstetrics & Gynaecology.
